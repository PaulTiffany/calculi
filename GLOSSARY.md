# Glossary

## abstraction
In λ-calculus, construction of a function-like term such as \(\lambda x.M\).

## application
Applying one λ-term to another, written \(MN\).

## β-reduction
The core λ-calculus rewrite:
\[
(\lambda x.M)N\to M[x:=N].
\]

## bisimulation
A behavioral equivalence used in process calculi: roughly, related processes can match one another's observable transitions according to the chosen definition.

## bounded observer
In the Fuzzy Calculus / *Principia Symbolica* context, an observer equipped with limited perceptual structure such as kernel, derivation, and bounded domain. Do not assume this term has the same definition in other literatures.

## calculus
A formal practice of objects, operations, and transformation/inference rules.

## calculus of indications
Spencer-Brown's distinction-centered formal system developed in *Laws of Form*.

## distinction graph
Goertzel's graph whose nodes are connected when a particular observer cannot distinguish them.

## encoding
A formal translation between calculi. An encoding must state what properties it preserves. Encoding does not imply identity.

## holonomy
Failure to return exactly to the original state after transport around a closed loop. Used in geometry; any use in another calculus must specify the transport structure.

## name
In process calculi, an entity that can identify communication structure. Exact semantics vary by calculus.

## normal form
A formal object to which no reduction of the relevant kind applies.

## observer-relative
Indexed to, conditioned on, or constructed from a specified observer. This phrase does not itself tell us whether the observer is primitive or derived.

## π-calculus
A process calculus centered on communication of names and dynamically changing connectivity.

## process calculus
A formalism for describing and reasoning about interacting concurrent processes.

## reduction
A formal rewrite relation. Different calculi define different reductions.

## residue
In the Fuzzy Calculus context, structured mismatch left by observer-relative differentiation/integration or transport. It should not be conflated automatically with numerical error.

## ρ-calculus
Reflective Higher-Order Calculus: a π-like reflective process calculus with structured names and reflective operators.

## sequent
An inferential object typically written
\[
\Gamma\vdash\Delta.
\]

## structural analogy
A similarity of formal pattern without a claim of ancestry or encoding.

## λ-calculus
A foundational calculus of abstraction, application, and substitution/reduction.
